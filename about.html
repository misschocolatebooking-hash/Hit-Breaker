What is Hit Breaker?

Hit Breaker is a skill-based arcade rhythm game created by Miss Chocolate — known as "The Hit Breaker" — a TV host and producer, radio personality, and event MC who has been breaking hit records since she was five years old. The game brings that same ear for music to your phone: tap the beats in time with the music, build combos, and climb the leaderboard.

Hit Breaker has two ways to play. The rhythm game offers daily tournaments with $1, $5, and $10 entry fees, where the top scorers split the prize pot. Hit High Breaker is our free Spades card game: watch a short ad, play a full game of Spades against three AI partners and opponents, and earn $0.02 per ad you watch.

Who is Miss Chocolate?

Miss Chocolate, also known as Chocolate, is the founder of Chocolate Deluxe Entertainment and the host and producer of the Artists vs Comedians TV Show. She has worked in music, radio, television, and live events since 1983, and was mentored by radio legend Cy Young of K97.5 FM in Raleigh, North Carolina, and by Charles Geer, known as "The Hit Maker," the first Black A&R executive and Head of Promotion at Atlantic Records and Warner Bros. Records. Her tagline is "Breaking Hits. Building Legends."

Hit Breaker grew out of that life in music. The rhythm game is built around timing, listening, and feel — the same skills that go into breaking a hit record on the radio. The Spades game grew out of the card tables and cookouts where Spades is a way of life: bidding, teamwork, and reading your partner.

How the games work

In the rhythm game, notes fall down four lanes. Tap the matching key — D, F, J, K on desktop, or tap the lanes on mobile — right as each note crosses the line. A Perfect hit (within 70 milliseconds) scores 100 points; a Good hit (within 150 milliseconds) scores 50 points. Combos multiply your score, up to 3x. Earn at least one star — 40% accuracy — to unlock the next level, and you can retry instantly if you miss the mark. There is no harsh game-over: the challenge comes from the score you need, not from punishment.

In Hit High Breaker Spades, you play with Marcus as your partner against Lisa and Dre. Each hand, every player bids on how many tricks their team will take, then plays five hands. Spades are always trump. Make your bid and you score 10 points per trick bid, plus one point per overtrick; miss it and you lose 10 points per trick bid. The team with the higher score after five hands wins.

How players earn

Tournament players pay a small entry fee — $1, $5, or $10 — and the winners split the pot: 50% for first place, 30% for second, and 20% for third. Winnings are paid through PayPal or Cash App, using the payout details you set in your profile.

Free players earn from advertising instead. Each ad you watch in Free Play earns $0.02, and your balance builds up until you reach the $5.00 cashout minimum. Payouts for ad earnings are processed once a month, after Google sends the ad revenue for the month — that is how the free game stays free and still pays players. Tournament winnings are separate from ad earnings and are paid on the tournament schedule.

Fair play and responsible play

Hit Breaker is a skill-based game: your score depends on your timing and accuracy, not on chance. Scores are checked on the server for impossible note counts, impossible speeds, and mismatched accuracy before they are saved, and every tournament game requires a one-time token issued when you start. Players must be 18 or older to enter paid tournaments. Play responsibly, and never spend more than you can afford on entry fees.
