HIT BREAKER by Miss Chocolate

How to Play

Two games, one site — rhythm tournaments and free Spades.

Getting started

Create a free account from the sign-up page with a username, email, and password. Logging in saves your scores, your tournament entries, and your ad earnings. In your profile, add your PayPal email or your Cash App tag and choose your payout method — that is where winnings and ad cashouts go.

The rhythm game

Controls. On desktop, use the keys D, F, J, and K — one for each of the four lanes. On mobile, tap the lanes directly. Notes fall down the lanes toward the hit line at the bottom of the screen. Tap the matching lane just as the note crosses the line.

Scoring. A Perfect hit — within 70 milliseconds of the note — scores 100 points. A Good hit — within 150 milliseconds — scores 50 points. Missing a note scores nothing and breaks your combo. Hitting notes in a row builds a combo, and your combo multiplies your points, up to 3x.

Levels and stars. Each level is a song with a set number of notes. At the end of a level you earn up to three stars based on your accuracy. You need at least one star — 40% accuracy — to unlock the next level. If you do not reach one star, you can retry right away; there is no game-over screen and no penalty beyond the retry.

Daily tournaments. Every day there are three tournaments: $1, $5, and $10 entry. Pay the entry fee with PayPal, play your game, and your best score is entered. At the end of the day the pot is split between the top three scorers: 50% for first, 30% for second, 20% for third. Check the leaderboard to see where you stand, and the tournaments page to enter.

Hit High Breaker — Spades

The table. You play with Marcus as your partner against Lisa and Dre, all controlled by the computer. The game is five hands long. Spades are always trump, which means a spade beats any card of another suit.

Bidding. At the start of each hand, look at your 13 cards and bid how many tricks you think your team — you plus Marcus — will take. A good starting point: count your high spades (Jack or higher) and your aces. Your partner bids too, and your two bids are added together as your team bid. The other team does the same.

Playing a trick. The player who leads a trick plays any card. The other players must follow the suit that was led if they can; if they cannot, they may play a spade to trump it, or discard another card. The highest card of the suit led wins the trick, unless a spade was played — then the highest spade wins. The winner of each trick leads the next one. You cannot lead with a spade until spades have been "broken" — played on an earlier trick — unless spades are all you have left.

Scoring a hand. If your team takes at least as many tricks as you bid, you score 10 points for every trick you bid, plus 1 point for every extra trick (an overtrick). If you fall short, you lose 10 points for every trick you bid. After five hands, the team with the higher total score wins the game.

Free Play and earnings. Spades is free to play: watch a short ad on the Free Play page to unlock a game, and you earn $0.02 for each ad you watch. Your balance is shown on the Free Play page. Once you reach $5.00 you can request a cashout to PayPal or Cash App. Ad payouts are processed once a month, after Google sends the ad revenue — see the FAQ for the full payout schedule.

Tips

For the rhythm game, listen to the beat rather than watching only the notes — your timing will be steadier. Start on the lower levels and build your accuracy before chasing high combos. For Spades, bid conservatively when your hand is weak, protect your high spades, and watch what your partner plays: their lead tells you what they are strong in.
