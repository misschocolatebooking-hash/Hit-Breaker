What to include

To help us help you quickly, please include:

Account problems: the username and email address on your account, and what happens when you try to log in.
Payout questions: your username, whether it is a tournament win or an ad cashout, and the amount.
Bug reports: what you were doing when it happened, which game (rhythm or Spades), and whether you were on a phone or a computer.
Press and partnerships: your name, your outlet or company, and what you would like to cover or propose.
