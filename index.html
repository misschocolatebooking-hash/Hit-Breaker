Two ways to play

Hit Breaker rhythm game. Tap the beats in time with the music across four lanes. Perfect hits score 100 points, Good hits score 50, and combos multiply your score up to 3x. Earn at least one star — 40% accuracy — to unlock the next level, with instant retry and no harsh game-over. Every day there are three tournaments — $1, $5, and $10 entry — and the top three scorers split the pot 50/30/20.

Hit High Breaker Spades — free to play. Play a full game of Spades with Marcus as your partner against Lisa and Dre. Bid your tricks, play five hands, and win the table. Watch a short ad to unlock a game and earn $0.02 per ad, cashing out at $5.00. Ad payouts are processed once a month, after Google sends the ad revenue.

How it works
