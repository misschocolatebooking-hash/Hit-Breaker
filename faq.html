Frequently Asked Questions

Is Hit Breaker free to play?

Yes. The Spades game, Hit High Breaker, is free: watch a short ad and you unlock a full game. The rhythm game also has free play. Paid daily tournaments are optional — $1, $5, or $10 entry — and only for players who want to compete for a cash prize pot.

How do I earn money playing?

There are two separate ways. In paid tournaments, the entry fees form a prize pot, and the top three scorers split it: 50% for first place, 30% for second, 20% for third. In free play, you earn $0.02 for each ad you watch, and that balance builds up until you can cash out. Tournament winnings and ad earnings are separate — they are not mixed together.

When do ad earnings pay out?

Payouts for ad earnings are processed once a month, after Google sends the ad revenue for the month. That means your cashout request is paid in the monthly payout, not instantly. This keeps the free game honest: players are paid from real ad revenue once it actually arrives, not from money the site does not have yet. Tournament winnings are paid on the tournament schedule instead.

What is the cashout minimum?

$5.00. You need at least $5.00 in ad earnings before you can request a cashout. At $0.02 per ad, that is 250 ads watched. Your balance and ad count are shown on the Free Play page.

How do I get paid?

Add your PayPal email or your Cash App tag in your profile, and choose which one you want to use. PayPal payouts are sent to the email you set; Cash App payouts are sent manually to your tag by the site admin. Make sure your payout details are correct — payouts sent to the wrong address cannot be recovered.

Do I have to be 18 to play?

You must be 18 or older to enter paid tournaments. Free play is open to everyone, but players under 18 should have a parent or guardian's permission, and only adults can request cashouts.

Is Hit Breaker a game of skill or chance?

Skill. In the rhythm game your score depends on how accurately you tap notes in time with the music. In Spades your result depends on how you bid and play your cards. Scores are verified on the server: impossible note counts, impossible speeds, and accuracy that does not match the reported hits are rejected.

Why do I need an account?

Your account saves your scores, your tournament entries, your leaderboard position, and your ad earnings. Without an account, none of that can be tracked or paid out. Your password is stored as a secure hash — it is never stored in plain text and staff cannot see it.

What if I forget my password?

Use the password reset link on the login page, or contact us from the contact page with the email address on your account and we will help you get back in.

Why does the ad say it is simulated?

While the site is waiting for Google AdSense approval, the ad shown in Free Play is a placeholder, not a real paid ad. Your $0.02-per-view credit is still recorded, and it will be paid out in the monthly payout once real ad revenue is coming in. Once AdSense approves the site, real ads will replace the placeholder.
