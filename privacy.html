Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

What we collect

When you create an account, we collect your username, your email address, and your password. Your password is stored as a secure hash and is never stored in plain text. If you want to receive payouts, you may also provide a PayPal email address or a Cash App tag, and choose your preferred payout method. When you play, we record your game scores, your tournament entries, and the ads you watch, so we can run leaderboards, run tournaments, and pay out earnings.

How we use it

We use your information to run the game: to log you in, to save and display your scores, to enter you into tournaments you pay for, to calculate prize payouts, and to track and pay ad earnings. We use your payout details only to send you money you have won or earned. We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

Advertising

This site uses Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings page. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet. For more information, see Google's Privacy & Terms page and the AdSense programme policies.

Our authorised ad seller is listed in our ads.txt file: google.com, pub-6967418064069348, DIRECT.

Cookies and sessions

We use a session cookie to keep you logged in while you play. It lasts up to seven days and is not used for advertising. Google AdSense may set its own cookies for advertising purposes, as described above.

Children

Paid tournaments are for players aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete the account.

Your choices

You can update your payout details at any time from your profile page. You can ask us to delete your account and your personal information by contacting us from the contact page. Deleting your account also removes any unpaid balance, so please cash out first if you have earnings above the $5.00 minimum.

Contact
